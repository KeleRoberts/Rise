The Book

What is Rise Above really about?

It’s not a manual that tells you what to believe. It’s a conversation about race, culture, religion, karma, the body, forgiveness, and the quiet revolution that happens when you stop abandoning your own antenna.

Early working title · Rise Above

Rise Above traces the subtle movement from unconscious survival into conscious living. It looks at how we inherit stories from family, culture, religion and history, and how those stories shape our sense of self, our relationships, and our understanding of the world and God.

Through personal reflection, spiritual insight and grounded honesty, the book explores themes such as racial identity, the weight of expectation, spiritual guilt, the cycles we call “karma”, and the tender work of accountability and forgiveness.

Rather than offering neat answers, Rise Above invites you to witness your own life with new eyes, to see where you’ve been living on reaction mode, and where something deeper in you is quietly asking to be awakened.

“To rise above is not to escape the world, but to experience it from clarity.”
~ Kelethabetse Roberts

Themes

If these questions keep you up at night, you’re in the right place

The book is anchored in a few repeating questions: Who am I really? How do I hold both human limitation and divine possibility? How do I embody the God within me fully without fear and religious guilt?

Karma & accountability

Not “cosmic punishment,” but the echo of choices: both personal and collective. This book explores how repeating patterns in our lives invite us to step into responsibility and break free from the victim mindset, moving from blame toward the realization that we are, indeed, masters of our own fate, in the words of the very wise Nelson Mandela.

Faith without bypassing reality

How do we speak of God, hope and heaven without ignoring injustice, trauma or the body? How do we forgive and express compassion but still allow consequences of actions to take effect? Rise Above wrestles with this tension: spirituality that doesn’t ask you to pretend.

The body as a sacred vehicle

From melanin to hormones to nervous system responses, the text reflects on how biology, ancestry, and geography meet within the body, and why listening to it is, in itself, a spiritual act. It considers the understanding that every human is a living expression of the word of God, without exception, and how this recognition may serve as a path toward collective liberation.

Rising in everyday life

Rising above requires a deep dive into one’s own psyche: the recognition of toxic patterns, the acknowledgment of the role you play in sustaining them, and the release of ideologies that no longer serve you or those around you. Above all, it demands the courage to refuse the abandonment of your truth for approval.

The Author

Who is behind Rise Above?

An observer of systems, people and patterns. Someone who has lived in multiple cultures and carries questions more than conclusions.

Kelethabetse “Kelly” Roberts
Explorer of consciousness · The Sage archetype · Bridge between logic & spirit

Kelethabetse has always carried a wandering spirit. Even as a child, her thoughts drifted toward spaces beyond the environment she inhabited. The first question she ever asked herself was why humans looked different, followed closely by why those who did not share her skin color seemed to hold an advantage. She observed that the environment she was born into was not as conducive as that of her white counterparts. This was not resentment, but childhood curiosity and innocence at play. Her questions extended far beyond race. She wondered about different countries, nationalities, and cultures. Beneath it all, she carried an unsettling sense that something was not quite right with the world, though she lacked the language to articulate it.

As she grew, so did her curiosity, and with it, an insatiable need to understand. This inner drive quietly guided her toward the path she walks today. As an adult, amid questioning her role within the broader spectrum of existence, she realized she had been walking this path her entire life. Not consciously. Not intentionally. But organically, through the architecture of her own nature. The questioning and wandering were not coincidences, but the way in which the divine chose to express its vastness through her. She came to see that her path was not beginning anew; it was a return to its origin.

Rise Above was born from seasons of upheaval, relocation, cultural mirrors, faith questions and the raw fear of leaving this world without ever sharing what lives in her heart. The book is her way of saying: “I was here. I paid attention. And I chose to let what I saw become something healing.” She does not claim to have all the answers as she is still learning herself. Her writing aims to be a catalyst for inner revolution. And to merge the knowledge of the sages of the past with her own insight.

There is a wise saying: you cannot keep doing the same thing and expect different results. Collectively, we have accepted ideologies about who we are and continue to play the same script on repeat. This is a call for redirection, an invitation to see differently, to question more deeply, and to unlearn what no longer serves. Kelethabetse invites all to Rise Above.
